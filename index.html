Experience

Hi, I'm a UX/UI Designer with several years of experience in B2B systems, complex workflows, and conversion-driven experiences. My background is in visual communication, and I use it alongside strategic UX thinking to build products that stay clear and usable as they grow.

I've led design processes end to end, from research and information architecture through to high-fidelity UI and design systems, with the focus on usability, clarity and CRO.

I came into tech after leaving a strict Haredi background, and I got here mostly by teaching myself. I work in Figma, and day to day with Claude, Midjourney, Gemini and AI-driven workflows. I'm still learning, and I'd rather do it on products that matter.

A glimpse of me and my world

When I'm not designing, I'm usually at a piano somewhere, chasing a melody until it finally sits right.

The rest of the time I'm buried in some new tool or idea, taking things apart to see how they work. Music keeps me patient; curiosity keeps me building.
