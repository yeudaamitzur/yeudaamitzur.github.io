TrailDesk, an app for finding a public place you can actually work from

70% of remote workers face difficulties while working from home

TrailDesk

Platform
workspace discovery app
Role
UX/UI · Branding
Timeline
2 months
Tools
Figma

Problem

Most remote workers face difficulties while working from home due to noise, distractions, and a lack of separation between work and personal life.

Many want to work outside the home but avoid it due to uncertainty about conditions in public workspaces. Currently, there is no solution that aggregates a wide range of public spaces with reliable, up-to-date information on their conditions.

Solution

Provide a platform that aggregates a wide range of public workspaces with reliable, up-to-date information on conditions — including comfortable seating, Wi-Fi quality, power availability, noise levels, parking, and more.

The insights came from surveys and interviews.

82%

Participants report that they often seek a change in their work location to feel more productive and creative

74%

Participants report taking intentional breaks very often to cope with distractions

68%

Participants report not using platforms to find public remote workspaces due to limited availability

59%

Participants report asking friends or searching for public workspaces on social media

Market overview

All of these and more focus on very expensive coworking spaces or generic coffee shops.
